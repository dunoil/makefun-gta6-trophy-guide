One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy any guide flags as potentially missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning: she says she is going to meditate and walks to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. That meditation is gone after Chapter 1, and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it, so do it to be safe. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Source: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.
